Bridging Classroom & Career

From the classroom to the industry.

Career choices become easier to imagine when students can see the people doing the work. Industry Takeover gives students direct access to practitioners and industry experts who share their experiences, explain the realities of their fields, and open up conversations that don't always happen in the lecture hall.

An Eightplus initiative designed to help students and emerging professionals see where their education can take them — and meet the people shaping the field today.

What careers exist within communications?

From strategic comms and PR to journalism, digital, creative, corporate affairs, public affairs, and research.

What does the work actually involve?

Because the job description rarely tells the whole story of daily industry execution.

What skills matter once you enter?

Technical skills matter. So do judgment, curiosity, writing, research, collaboration, and human understanding.

How do you get started?

Honest dialogue on entering the industry, building experience, finding opportunities, and navigating early careers.

Where is the profession going?

Navigating how AI, data, technology, and evolving media landscapes are actively reshaping our field.

Flexible Session Formats

What an Industry Takeover Can Look Like

A conversation built around your students. Every Industry Takeover is custom-shaped around the host institution, student body, and specific academic theme.

Careers in Communications

An introduction to the full breadth of careers available across communications, media, corporate affairs, public relations, digital, and creative industries.

The Real World of Work

Practitioners share what their work actually involves day-to-day, what they wish they had known earlier, and what employers look for beyond academic qualifications.

The Future of Communication

A forward-looking conversation about AI, data, technology, changing audience dynamics, and the modern capabilities future professionals need.

From Student to Professional

Practical guidance on building experience, developing a personal professional identity, finding opportunities, and successfully making the transition into the workplace.

Industry Spotlights

Focused sessions bringing specialists from specific sectors — such as mining, finance, tech, media, or creative industries — into direct conversation with students.
