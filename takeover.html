Bridging classroom and career

From the classroom to the industry.

Career choices become easier to imagine when students can see the people doing the work.

Industry Takeover gives students direct access to practitioners and industry experts who share their experiences, explain the realities of their fields, and open conversations that do not always happen in the lecture hall.

It is an Eightplus initiative designed to help students and emerging professionals see where their education can take them — and meet the people shaping the field today.

What careers exist within communications?

From strategic communications and PR to journalism, digital, creative, corporate affairs, public affairs and research.

What does the work actually involve?

Because the job description rarely tells the whole story of daily industry execution.

What skills matter once you enter?

Technical skills matter. So do judgement, curiosity, writing, research, collaboration and human understanding.

How do you get started?

Honest conversations about entering the industry, building experience, finding opportunities and navigating early careers.

Where is the profession going?

Exploring how AI, data, technology and changing media landscapes are reshaping the field.

Flexible session formats

What an Industry Takeover can look like.

A conversation built around your students. Every Industry Takeover is shaped around the host institution, student body, academic theme and the practitioners best placed to contribute.

01 · Careers

Careers in communications

An introduction to the breadth of careers across communications, media, corporate affairs, public relations, digital and creative industries.

02 · Practice

The real world of work

Practitioners share what their work actually involves, what they wish they had known earlier and what employers look for beyond qualifications.

03 · Future

The future of communication

A forward-looking conversation about AI, data, technology, changing audiences and the capabilities future professionals will need.

04 · Transition

From student to professional

Practical guidance on building experience, developing a professional identity, finding opportunities and entering the workplace.

05 · Sector

Industry spotlights

Focused sessions bringing specialists from sectors such as mining, finance, technology, media or creative industries into direct conversation with students.

Institutional impact

More than a career talk.

Students need more than general inspiration. They need access, context and honest information. Industry Takeover is built around real practitioners and candid conversations that help students connect what they are learning with where it can take them.

For institutions, it is a practical way to bring current industry knowledge into the learning environment, strengthen professional exposure and connect students with people actively shaping the field.
