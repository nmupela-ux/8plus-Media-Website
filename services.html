Capabilities & Expertise

Strategic communications, built around what matters.

Communication is rarely just about saying the right thing. It is about knowing what to say, when to say it, who needs to hear it — and what is at stake if you get it wrong. Eightplus brings strategic counsel, editorial thinking, and creative execution together to help organisations build trust, manage complexity, and communicate with purpose.

Specialist Disciplines

Corporate Communications

Build a reputation that works as hard as your business does.

We shape the narratives, messages, and communication strategies that define how organisations are understood by the people who matter most.

Crisis Communications

When the pressure is on, clarity matters.

We help organisations prepare for, respond to, and recover from reputational challenges — bringing structure, speed, and strategic counsel when it matters most.

Employee Communications

Your people are your first audience.

From organisational change to culture and leadership communication, we help businesses keep employees informed, engaged, and aligned.

Media & Executive Training

Give leaders the confidence to lead the conversation.

We prepare executives and spokespeople to communicate clearly and credibly — from media interviews and public appearances to high-stakes conversations.

Performance Content & Creative

Make your message worth paying attention to.

From campaigns and editorial content to digital and social creative, we turn strategy into communication that earns attention and moves audiences to act.

Stakeholder & Reputation Management

Understand the room before you enter it.

We help organisations identify, understand, and engage the audiences that shape their reputation — from customers and communities to regulators, partners, and the media.

ESG Communications & Impact Reporting

Turn impact into something people can understand and trust.

We help organisations communicate sustainability, social impact, and responsible business commitments with clarity, substance, and credibility.

Experiential Event Management & Strategy

Create experiences that people remember.

From executive roundtables to corporate events and stakeholder engagements, we design experiences that bring strategy, people, and purpose together.

Sectors

Communication doesn't happen in a vacuum. Every sector has its own pressures, audiences, and rules of engagement. We bring communications expertise with an understanding of the environments our clients operate in.

Financial Services

Trust is the business.

Financial institutions operate in a highly regulated, highly scrutinised environment where one poorly handled message can quickly become a reputational issue. We help banks, fintechs, insurers, and investment firms navigate complex stakeholder environments, communicate with confidence, and build trust across customers, regulators, investors, and the public.

Mining & Extractives

Your licence to operate is built on trust.

Mining sits at the intersection of business, government, communities, investors, and the environment. We help extractive-sector organisations communicate their value, manage stakeholder relationships, navigate scrutiny, and tell credible stories about their economic, social, and environmental impact.

Technology & Fintech

Make innovation understandable. Make it trusted.

Technology moves quickly. Regulation, public expectations, and scrutiny are moving with it. We help technology and fintech businesses translate complex products and ideas into clear, credible narratives — while building the trust needed to scale.

FMCG & Consumer Brands

Attention gets you noticed. Trust keeps you chosen.

Consumer brands compete in crowded markets where reputation, relevance, and consistency matter. We help brands connect with audiences through strategic storytelling, creative campaigns, media relations, and communications that turn attention into affinity.

Non-Profit & Development

Communicate impact without losing the human story.

Development organisations operate across donors, partners, communities, governments, media, and the public. We help organisations communicate programmes and impact with clarity — building visibility, credibility, and support while keeping the people behind the numbers at the centre.

Creative & Cultural Industries

Ideas deserve audiences.

Creative organisations and cultural ventures need more than visibility — they need positioning, audiences, and a story people want to be part of. We help creative businesses, initiatives, and institutions turn ideas into compelling narratives and meaningful public presence.

One approach. Eight specialist disciplines. Multiple sectors.

Our work is grounded in the Wholly Aligned Framework™ — connecting strategy, reputation, storytelling, and execution so that every communication works towards the same objective.

Strategy → Narrative → Engagement → Execution → Impact

Be understood. Be trusted. Be chosen.
