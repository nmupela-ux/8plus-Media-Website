PAYZ

Building a clearer voice for Zambia’s payments ecosystem

Six-Month Engagement

PAYZ operates within a fast-moving financial and payments ecosystem where clarity, credibility, and visibility matter. The challenge was not simply to produce more content, but to create a more coherent communications presence that reflected PAYZ’s role and ambitions.

The Challenge

PAYZ needed to strengthen how it presented itself publicly—from its visual identity and messaging to the way it communicated its work, perspectives, and role within the payments ecosystem.

The engagement called for a more consistent and purposeful communications approach across platforms and audiences.

The Approach

Eightplus Media led a six-month communications transformation engagement spanning strategy, brand expression, and content. Rather than treating each communication as a standalone deliverable, we created a connected communications system where brand, messaging, and content reinforced one another.

Refining communications positioning & messaging
Developing a refreshed visual identity
Establishing social media content pillars
Developing platform-specific editorial content
Producing thought-leadership articles
Supporting campaigns & stakeholder communications
Developing newsletter content & print materials
Providing ongoing PR & communications strategy support

The Result

The engagement gave PAYZ a clearer and more consistent public identity, strengthened its communications infrastructure, and created a stronger foundation for visibility within Zambia’s evolving payments ecosystem. The work moved PAYZ from simply communicating activity to communicating with greater clarity, consistency, and strategic intent.

Scope: Strategy Brand & Messaging Social & Digital Content Thought Leadership Campaign Support Stakeholder Comms Newsletter Development

Strategic messaging & communication
