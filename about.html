About Eightplus

Strategic communication for organisations navigating complexity.

Eightplus Media is an independent strategic communications consultancy based in Zambia. We help organisations communicate with greater clarity, coherence, and credibility — particularly where reputation, stakeholder relationships, sustainability, and public trust matter.

Our work sits between strategy and execution. We bring together strategic counsel, editorial thinking, creative production, and local understanding to help organisations navigate the moments that matter.

Advisory-led Editorially precise Locally fluent

WHY EIGHTPLUS

Communication is more than output.

Organisations communicate constantly — through their leaders, employees, products, policies, customer interactions, media coverage, and the decisions they make.

We believe effective communication starts before the message. It starts with understanding the context, the audience, the problem, and the outcome.

That is why our work begins with alignment: bringing organisational intent, stakeholder expectations, and communication into closer relationship.

HOW WE WORK

Strategy should not end when the campaign launches.

01 / THINK

THINK

We investigate the context before jumping to execution. What is happening? Who matters? What are they saying? What does the evidence tell us?

02 / ALIGN

ALIGN

We connect objectives, audiences, messages, and actions so that communication is working towards something meaningful.

03 / CREATE

CREATE

We turn strategy into clear narratives, content, campaigns, experiences, and tools that people can actually engage with.

04 / LEARN

LEARN

OUR PEOPLE

NDULWA MUPELA

Founder & Strategic Communications Lead

Ndulwa began her communications career as a writer and has spent the past decade working across writing and editing, agency environments, freelance consulting, and strategic communications leadership.

Her work spans strategic communications, stakeholder engagement, sustainability and impact reporting, internal communications, public-facing campaigns, and executive communications.

She founded Eightplus to build a communications practice that treats communication as a leadership and organisational function, rather than simply an output.

Her particular interest lies in the space where strategy, people, reputation, and impact intersect — helping organisations understand not only what they need to say, but what needs to be understood, aligned, and acted upon.
