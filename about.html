Why We Built It

Communication should do more than fill channels.

"The message is rarely the whole problem."

Organisations are constantly communicating — through leaders, employees, customers, products, policies, decisions, media coverage and the experiences people have with them.

Yet communication is often treated as an output problem: another campaign, another post, another press release, another presentation.

Eightplus was built around a different view. The real work starts before the message.

We want to understand what is happening, what the organisation is trying to achieve, who needs to move, what people already believe, and where the gaps are between intent and experience. From there, communication becomes more useful: not simply something an organisation produces, but something that helps people understand, trust and act.

Our Approach

Wholly Aligned.

Good communication does not sit separately from the organisation producing it. It connects leadership intent, business priorities, organisational reality, stakeholder expectations and the way people actually experience the organisation.

What Makes The Work Different

Context over templates.

No two organisations arrive with the same problem. The sector matters. The market matters. The history matters. The audience matters. The politics, culture, timing and lived experience around an organisation matter too.

So we don't start with a template and look for somewhere to put the organisation. We start with the situation. That means research, listening, questioning, evidence and judgement before deciding what communication should look like.

How We Work

Senior thinking stays close to the work.

We don't separate strategy from execution and hand a brief down a production line. The people shaping the thinking stay connected to how the work is developed, made and delivered.

We ask questions early. We challenge assumptions. We use evidence where it is available and judgement where it isn't. And we bring in specialist expertise when the work needs it.

The result should be work that is not only strategically sound, but usable in the real world.

What We Believe

A few things we won't compromise on.

01

CLARITY

Clarity is not simplification. It is knowing what matters, what doesn't, and what people actually need to understand.

02

CONTEXT

Context changes the answer. What works in one organisation, market or moment may be completely wrong in another.

03

EVIDENCE

Evidence should shape strategy. We want to know what people are doing, saying and experiencing — not just what we hope is true.

04

USEFULNESS

Good ideas have to survive contact with reality. Beautiful strategy that cannot be implemented is not much of a strategy.

Where We're Rooted

Built in Zambia. Looking outward.

Eightplus is rooted in Zambia — in its businesses, institutions, industries, people and particular ways of working.
